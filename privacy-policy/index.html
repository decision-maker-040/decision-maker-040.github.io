Spin the Wheel: Decision Maker

Privacy Policy · Effective Date: October 8, 2026

Spin the Wheel: Decision Maker ("Spin the Wheel", "we", "our", or "us") is a random decision-making application designed to help users make quick and fun choices using customizable wheels and random selection tools.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Spin the Wheel. We aim to collect and use information only as reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the application and its features.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Spin the Wheel may process certain information necessary to operate the application.

🎡 Wheel Choices Names, options, activities, foods, places, or other entries that you add to a custom wheel.
⚙️ App Usage Information Information about interactions with features may be processed to understand and improve the application.
📱 Device Information Basic technical information may be processed for compatibility, diagnostics, security, and application functionality.
📊 Diagnostic Information Technical information related to crashes, errors, performance, or application stability may be processed.

2. Information You Enter

Spin the Wheel allows you to create custom wheels by entering your own choices, names, activities, foods, places, tasks, or other text.

This information is provided by you voluntarily when using the application's random selection features.

Depending on the application version and implementation, custom wheel data and preferences may be stored locally on your device so that you can continue using your saved wheels.

✨ Please avoid entering sensitive personal information into wheel entries or other optional fields within the application.

3. Spin the Wheel and Random Selection

The main purpose of Spin the Wheel is to provide random selection tools. The application may process the options you provide in order to generate a random result.

  • Spin customizable decision wheels
  • Create random teams or groups
  • Select a random winner
  • Roll virtual dice
  • Flip a virtual coin
  • Use ready-made wheel templates

The random result is generated for the purpose of providing the requested feature and helping you make a decision.

4. Custom Wheels and Saved Data

You may create and customize wheels for different situations, including meals, games, activities, challenges, tasks, names, places, and other decisions.

Where supported, your custom wheels, options, and preferences may be stored locally on your device. Such locally stored information may remain available until you delete it, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or otherwise remove it through available application controls.

We do not require you to provide your real name, phone number, address, or other direct identifying information simply to create a random wheel.

5. Random Teams and Winner Selection

Spin the Wheel may allow you to enter participant names or other identifiers to create random teams, groups, or select a winner.

These entries are processed only as necessary to perform the random selection requested by you. You are responsible for ensuring that you have appropriate permission to enter information relating to other individuals.

🛡️ We recommend using only the minimum information necessary for random selection and avoiding sensitive personal information.
